## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) answered seven authored questions over one drawn scene and reported `sample-sanity` exact-match/ANLS for that image. This notebook keeps that scene and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real out-of-distribution VQA corpus read annotation-column-only with per-photograph pins, corpus-level metrics in the carried `metrics.py` including majority match, two non-neural baselines, a bounded fine-tuning of the answer decoder with the vision features cached, a per-category breakdown, the scene re-answered after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's 3 modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 8-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh clone lacks (the git-ignored `model.safetensors`), at the pinned revision; `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry; `BlipVQAPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`); no pickle is opened anywhere (the upstream `pytorch_model.bin` and `tf_model.h5` are not listed); no cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `pyarrow`, `safetensors` or `urllib`.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample's annotations are the `question_id`, `question`, `answers` and `category` columns of one object in the Hub dataset repository `lmms-lab-encoder/VizWiz-VQA` at the immutable revision `d428a2dae984f79cf1b9d99467dfa883e0c30686` (`data/val-00000-of-00005-7775fd61bc6a3d98.parquet`, 405,819,917 bytes, SHA-256 `62a5fddb…`): the declared size and SHA-256 are checked against the pins through the Hub's file metadata before any byte is read, only the parquet footer and those columns are fetched over HTTPS range requests through `pyarrow` (about 0.13 MB; the shard's 406 MB of images are never downloaded), and the decoded columns are refused unless their SHA-256 matches the pin. The 240 photographs are fetched one by one from `https://vizwiz.cs.colorado.edu/VizWiz_visualization_img/`, each pinned by size and SHA-256 in the carried `samples.py` (about 113 MB), refused on any mismatch, spaced by 0.25 s and retried a bounded number of times on transient errors. The pinned questions are cut **by image** into 140 / 40 / 60 (`check_split_disjoint` asserts no shared image id). The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the repository, the revision, the file, the byte counts, the image host and the CC BY 4.0 licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_annotations` + `fetch_images` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `pipe.answer` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the drawn scene (inference contract) → `constant_answer_baseline` + `question_prefix_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with the per-category breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `pipe.answer` + `evaluation_report` on the scene again (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `BlipVQAPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity check (export and reload) → one question about a drawn sign (Section 11).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** the answer is generated text with no score, probability or correctness signal; VQA accuracy, exact match, majority match and ANLS are corpus-level agreement with accepted answers (own implementations of the benchmark conventions); no acceptance threshold ships, and a learned `unanswerable` is a learned answer, not a calibrated refusal. The scene re-answered after adaptation is scored with a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the constant-answer and question-prefix baselines and the frozen model are scored on the same 60 test photographs as the adapted model, on all four metrics and per category; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one corpus with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly that `unanswerable` is the majority answer of 35 of the 60 test questions, so the constant answer scores well on every metric including majority match (0.583). The answerability breakdown (`answerability_breakdown` in the carried `metrics.py`) prints, per category and per majority answer, the share of `unanswerable` answers, the VQA accuracy they earn and the VQA accuracy of the other answers, and the notebook states the reading it supports: the adapted model's gain is the learned `unanswerable` convention, applied to most photographs, with no evidence of better answers to answerable questions (review VQA-M2). Under BYOD the constant answer is the training data's most frequent majority answer.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; within a run, validation (40 questions, noisy) selects the epoch and the test split is used only for the final evaluation and the reload-parity check. The default hyperparameters, however, were compared on a test split in the build record (model card), and the notebook says so in Sections 7 and 8 and in the interpretation: the default-settings test gain is labelled optimistic, not independent evidence (SPL7; review VQA-m3). The notebook the notebook states that images from few photographers or sessions must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `IMAGE_SIZE` (384), `MAX_QUESTION_CHARS` (256), `MAX_NEW_TOKENS` (32), `DEFAULT_MAX_NEW_TOKENS` (10), `DECODING`, the record bounds (at most 5,000; a BYOD dataset needs at least 12 records on 12 different images so that the split by image leaves 8 / 2 / 2), `MIN_ANSWERS` (1) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip (upload, or a zip or folder path in `BYOD_PATH`) is extracted member by member (no `extractall`) into a freshly emptied `work/byod/`, keeping sub-folders under a guarded root (links and members outside the folder refused; at most 20,000 members and 2 GB expanded, checked before anything is written), a missing records file or an empty upload stops with a message, the minimum is checked in Section 4 naming the split, and its records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected schema and the privacy guidance — photographs of people, documents or homes with their questions are restricted data — are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split is written to `outputs/blip_vqa_train.jsonl` in the JSONL shape. Under BYOD `result.json` records the upload in a `byod` block and carries no VizWiz `corpus` block.
- **Runtime (RUN1, RUN10, ENV6):** nothing is installed into the notebook kernel. Section 1 downloads the pinned `uv` 0.12.15 wheel (refused unless its size and SHA-256 match), creates a managed CPython 3.12.12 environment and installs the carried hash-locked `tutorials/requirements-colab.lock.txt` (48 packages, compiled from the `pyproject.toml` pins for manylinux x86_64) with `--require-hashes --only-binary :all:`; every later cell runs in one persistent worker in that environment. The notebook therefore supports **Linux x86_64 runtimes only**.
- **Re-runs (UX7, RUN9, OUT8):** Sections 5, 6 and 7 reload the pretrained model when an earlier pass adapted it (`reset_to_pretrained()`), Section 6 refuses to report an adapted model as frozen, `pipe.adapt` refuses an already-adapted pipeline, reload parity stops with an explanation instead of a bare assertion, and Sections 6 and 8 print comparisons and a verdict (including *the selector kept epoch 0*) and a run history instead of asserting a result. Each optional experiment names the cell to change and the cell to **Run after**.
- **Guided layer (§3.5, GDL1–GDL15):** audience, input → model → output table, how to use, roadmap, observable objectives, a prediction before each learner section with a *What to notice* note and a collapsible worked answer after it, a change-one-thing activity (Section 10, `LEARNING_RATE = 1e-7`), a provided cell for a question about written text (Section 11), troubleshooting, glossary and a conclusion template; Sections 1–3 are labelled infrastructure and the carried code is collapsed.
